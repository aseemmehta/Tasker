Privacy policy

Session Ledger (also called Tasker) · Last updated 28 November 2026

Session Ledger is a small time tracker for repeatable tasks. It is run by me. You can reach me at aseem.max@gmail.com. The app is a static website: it has no server of its own and no user accounts.

The short version

  • Your tasks, sessions and goals stay with you: in your browser, in a file on your computer, or in your own Google Drive. I cannot see them.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you click “Allow”, and only counts page views. It is not linked to your Google sign-in or to your tasks.
  • I do not sell data, show ads, or use your data for advertising.

Data you create in the app

Task names, descriptions, goals and recorded sessions are stored where you choose:

  • Browser cache (the default): in your browser’s local storage on your device.
  • A local JSON file (Chrome and Edge): a file you pick on your computer.
  • Google Drive: a JSON data file in your own Drive.

This data is never sent to me or to any server I run. You can export it as a JSON file at any time in Settings.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, the app asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It lets the app create and read only the data file it created or that you opened with it. The app cannot see your other files.

  • The access token Google gives your browser is kept in memory only and is sent only to Google’s APIs. It is not stored on any server.
  • The app remembers in your browser that Drive is connected, the ID of its data file, and, if you entered one, the OAuth client ID.
  • To disconnect, use Unlink in Settings, which also revokes the token. You can also remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your data file stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Session Ledger’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics (only if you allow it)

When you first open the app you are asked whether to allow anonymous usage statistics. Nothing is sent to Google Analytics until you click “Allow”. If you do:

  • Google Analytics 4 receives page views: the page address without any query string or hash, a fixed page title, your approximate location derived from your IP address by Google, and technical details such as browser, operating system, screen size and language.
  • Google sets cookies (_ga and _ga_…) holding a random identifier for your browser. It is not your Google account and is not linked to your sign-in.
  • No task names, descriptions, times, notes or Drive data are sent. Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off.

I use the statistics only to see how many people use the app and roughly where they are. Google keeps this data for a limited period set in the analytics settings. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy. Turning analytics off stops sending data and removes the cookies. Google acts as a provider for this service, see Google’s privacy policy.

Other services involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log visits as part of running the service, see GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its font from Google when you open it, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.

Cookies and local storage

Apart from the Google Analytics cookies described above (only after you allow them), the app uses your browser’s local storage for your data cache, your settings and your analytics choice. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

Your task data is yours and under your control: edit it, export it, or delete it in the app, your file or your Drive. For analytics, you can withdraw consent at any time. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because analytics data is tied to a random browser identifier, I generally cannot identify an individual in it. If you have a request, write to me.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

I will update this page when something changes and show the date at the top. If I add optional features that send data to a server, such as leaderboards, I will update this policy first and those features will be opt-in.
